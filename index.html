Save Me

A personal tool that saves WhatsApp contacts to a Google Sheet and Google Contacts.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Save Me is a small private application. It reads the display name and phone number of people who appear in the owner's own WhatsApp chats and records them as contacts, so they do not have to be typed in by hand.

What it does

  • Saves each contact under the name that person set for themself on WhatsApp
  • Records them in a Google Sheet in the owner's own Google Drive
  • Creates them as contacts in the owner's own Google Contacts
  • Identifies people by phone number, so nobody is saved twice
  • Numbers exact duplicate names — the second Jason becomes "Jason 2"

Who it is for

It is a single-user application, run by its owner on their own computer, for their own contacts. It is not a service, it is not offered to the public, and it has no accounts to sign up for.

No servers. Save Me runs entirely on the owner's own machine. There is no hosted backend, no database belonging to anyone else, and no third party that receives the data. Contacts go from WhatsApp to the owner's own Google account and nowhere else.

Google data it uses

Google SheetsCreates and writes one spreadsheet — the contact list
Google ContactsCreates contacts in the owner's account

Both are described in full in the Privacy Policy.
