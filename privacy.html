Privacy Policy

How Save Me handles information, including data from Google APIs.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

HomeTerms of Service

1. What Save Me is

Save Me is a private, single-user application run by its owner on their own computer. It saves contacts from the owner's own WhatsApp account into the owner's own Google Sheet and Google Contacts. It is not a public service, has no user accounts, and is not offered to anyone else.

2. Information it handles

Contact nameThe display name a person has set on WhatsApp, or their phone number where no name is set
Phone numberUsed as the identifier, so the same person is never saved twice
Business detailsFor WhatsApp Business accounts only: the verified business name, category, email and website that the business has chosen to publish
TimestampsWhen a contact was first and last seen

Save Me does not read, store, or transmit message content. It does not access photos, media, location, or call history.

3. Google user data

Save Me requests two Google API scopes, both applying only to the account that authorises it:

.../auth/spreadsheetsTo create one spreadsheet and write contacts to it
.../auth/contactsTo create contacts, and to read existing ones only so that duplicates are not created

Google data is used solely to provide the contact-saving feature the owner asked for. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never transferred to anyone, and never used to train any model.

Limited Use. Save Me's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Where the data is kept

Contacts are written to the authorising account's own Google Drive and Google Contacts. They are stored nowhere else.

Save Me runs on the owner's own computer. There is no hosted server, no external database, and no analytics. The only network connections it makes are to WhatsApp and to Google. Authorisation tokens are stored in a file on that computer and are never transmitted anywhere except to Google.

5. Sharing

None. No information is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. There is no third party involved.

6. Retention and deletion

Contacts remain in the owner's Google account until the owner deletes them, the same as any contact added by hand. Deleting the spreadsheet or the contacts removes the data.

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Withdrawing access stops Save Me immediately; contacts already saved stay in the account, under the owner's control.

7. Other people's information

The contacts saved are people who have messaged, or been messaged by, the owner's own WhatsApp account — the same information visible in the owner's phone. It is handled for the owner's personal contact-management purposes and is not published, shared, or used to contact anyone.

8. Children

Save Me is not directed at children and is not made available to them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
